Why it exists

Most “deal of the day” pages pad inflated list prices and sponsored placements. Deal-hunting communities already surface real discounts, but you have to wade through hundreds of posts. DealBots picks one clear, worth-buying deal each day and explains why it made the cut. For the checks behind that — fake “was” prices, the recent low, and coupon fine print — read how to tell if a sale is real. To read the chart those checks rely on, see how to read a price history chart. For the holiday weekend, see are Black Friday deals actually real? To compare the two named days, see Black Friday vs Cyber Monday. For a month-by-month look at when categories commonly go on sale, see the best time to buy almost anything.

How it works

  1. 1

    Read the day's posts

    A daily pass looks at trending posts from deal-hunter communities.

  2. 2

    Drop the unclear ones

    Posts with unclear pricing, discounts that don't match the posted price, or prices locked behind a membership or rebate are filtered out.

  3. 3

    Rank what's left

    Remaining posts are ranked by the size of the discount and how fast the post is gaining traction.

  4. 4

    Show one pick

    The extension popup shows the top deal with a short explanation of why it made the cut. The rest of the day's picks are on the deals page.

FAQ and privacy

Do I need an account?

No. DealBots has no login, sign-up, or profile.

Do you track me?

No. DealBots does not collect, store, or sell personal data. There is no analytics and no tracking. The extension fetches the same public deal feed for everyone. That request does not include your identity or browsing history, and the extension does not read the pages you visit. The full policy is on the privacy page.

Is it free?

Yes. Installing DealBots on Chrome, Edge, or Firefox costs nothing.

How often does the deal change?

Once a day. If a day has no current pick, the extension says today's pick is on its way and asks you to check back soon.

Where do the deals come from?

From public posts in deal-hunter communities. Prices and availability change quickly, so confirm the offer before you buy. The popup shows one pick; today's deals lists the rest.

Affiliate disclosure

Some product links in DealBots are affiliate links. If you buy through one of them, we may earn a commission. That does not change the price you pay, and it does not decide which deal is picked.
