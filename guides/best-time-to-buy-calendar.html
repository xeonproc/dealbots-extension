The best time to buy almost anything: a month-by-month guide (2026-2027)

Updated October 22, 2026

The best month is a pattern. The price you pay is the fact. TVs often get cheaper around the late-November shopping weekend. Winter coats often get cheaper when the season is ending. A calendar tells you when to look.

The notes below run from November 2026 through October 2027, and they describe common patterns in the United States. In 2026, Black Friday is Friday, November 27, and Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30. A category can skip a year. A popular size can sell out. A seller can print a sale badge on an ordinary price in the middle of the right month.

How to use this calendar

Pick the category, then the month, then the item. The month is a time to open a price history. If you need a specific size, color, or model soon, waiting for a later month can mean it is gone. End-of-season leftovers are often cheaper, and the choice is often thinner.

Holiday weekends are when mattresses and large appliances commonly carry a sale label. Where a month names a holiday, the date is for this 2026-2027 span. The habit — a long weekend, the end of a season, back-to-school, the weeks around a big game — is the part that tends to repeat. The label still has to beat prices from the weeks around it.

Month by month

November 2026 through October 2027. Each note is a common pattern for that kind of product. It is not a promise that the item in your cart will be cheap.

November 2026

TVs, headphones, laptops, tablets, and other electronics commonly go on sale around Black Friday and Cyber Monday, and in the days on either side of that weekend. In 2026, Black Friday is Friday, November 27, and Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30. The lowest price does not always land on the named day. Toys, gifts, and small kitchen appliances often join the same stretch.

Mattresses and large appliances sometimes show up on that holiday weekend too, and they also show up on other long weekends later in this calendar. Winter coats, boots, and snow gear are often still early in their season. November is a common time to buy them if you need them for this winter, and a common time to wait if you are only hoping for an end-of-season price. That clearance more often arrives in late winter.

December 2026

The first few weeks often carry November's gift pricing forward. Electronics, toys, and small appliances commonly stay on sale, and some prices dip again closer to the holiday. Christmas Day is Friday, December 25. A label that says the offer ends tonight can still be last week's price with a new headline.

After the holiday, leftover seasonal goods commonly start to clear: holiday decor, some toys, and clothing bought as gifts. Winter coats may ease. The deeper end-of-season cuts more often show up from January through March, when fewer people still need them. If a gift has to arrive before the holiday, whether the item is still available matters more than waiting for a later month.

January 2027

January is a common clearance month. New Year's Day is Friday, January 1. Holiday decor, unsold toys, and some small appliances often step down. Sheets, towels, and other bedding commonly go on sale. Fitness equipment often carries a sale label as people start new routines, and that same demand can keep a popular item from falling as far as the ad suggests.

Winter clothing sometimes dips, with more of the leftovers commonly waiting until February and March. TVs can move again ahead of major sporting events in February. January is less about one named weekend and more about what did not sell in December.

February 2027

TVs and home-theater gear often go on sale in the weeks before a major sporting event that people watch at home. The event is a reason the ads appear. The checkout price still has to beat the recent low. Compare that week's total with the weeks before the ads started.

Presidents Day, Monday, February 15, is a common window for mattresses, large appliances, and furniture. Winter coats, boots, and snow gear often move into end-of-season pricing as the cold eases, especially later in the month. The sizes that are left are the sizes you can buy. A popular size can already be gone.

March 2027

End-of-season winter gear often continues, and March is commonly one of the better months to look if you can store a coat or a pair of boots until next winter. The rack is thinner than it was in the fall. A lower price, with less choice, is the usual shape of end-of-season shopping.

Vacuums, storage, and other spring-cleaning gear often pick up. Outdoor furniture and grills are usually just arriving, so early spring is often a season of regular prices, with clearance more common at the end of summer. Easter is Sunday, March 28. The days around it sometimes bring clothing and home promotions. Candy and seasonal decor for that holiday often clear just after it.

April 2027

Lawn and garden tools, patio furniture, and grills are in their selling season. Prices are commonly firmer than they will be in late summer. Promotions show up, and they are often a sale label on an in-season price.

Spring clothing replaces what is left of winter. A coat still on the rack is often cheaper, in whatever sizes remain. Vacuums and other cleaning gear sometimes stay on a spring promotion that started in March. If you do not need the outdoor set in April, late summer is when this calendar comes back to it.

May 2027

Memorial Day is Monday, May 31. That long weekend is one of the regular windows for mattresses, large appliances, grills, and outdoor furniture. The pattern is common enough to plan a look around. It is still a headline until the checkout price beats recent weeks.

Early summer clothing, fans, and other cooling gear start to show up. Cooling gear often costs more once hot weather is already here, so a quieter week before a heat wave can be as useful as the holiday ad. Waiting until August can mean a clearance price and a thinner choice.

June 2027

Grills, outdoor furniture, and summer clothing are in season. Sale labels are common, and the deeper cuts more often wait until the season is ending. Father's Day is Sunday, June 20. Tools, grills, and some electronics often get an extra round of gift ads that week.

Small kitchen appliances sometimes go on sale around wedding season. A bundle or a "free" extra helps when you wanted every part of it. Divide the package price by what you will actually use. June is a reasonable month for summer gear you need this season, and a common month to wait on leftovers you can buy in August or September.

July 2027

July 4 falls on a Sunday, so the holiday weekend is the days around it. Mattresses, large appliances, and summer gear often carry sale labels then, in the same family as Memorial Day and Labor Day. Swimwear and summer clothes are still in season. Clearance on those commonly builds in August.

Midsummer is also when broad sale events often cluster, including online events that are not tied to a public holiday. The categories change from year to year. Electronics, home goods, and an early look at back-to-school gear sometimes show up. The event name is a headline. A July price can repeat a low from May or June.

August 2027

Laptops, tablets, backpacks, and dorm-room basics commonly go on sale for back-to-school. Some of those laptop prices return around the late-November weekend, so August is a strong month to look when the computer is for the school year that is about to start. It is not the only month a laptop goes on sale.

Summer clothing, pool gear, and some outdoor furniture often start end-of-season pricing. Grills sometimes join them. A patio set you can store is often cheaper in late summer than in May, with fewer choices left. A specific color or size can sell out while the category is still on sale.

September 2027

Labor Day is Monday, September 6. That weekend is a common window for mattresses, appliances, and outdoor furniture that did not sell over the summer. It is one of the last big warm-weather weekends, so leftover patio sets and grills often meet a holiday ad at the same time.

Back-to-school pricing on laptops and clothing often runs into the first weeks of the month, then fades once classes are underway. New television models often arrive in the fall. Last year's models sometimes drop when the new ones show up, and sometimes they do not. The wider TV discounts commonly wait for late November. A claim that a new model just arrived is a reason to open the price history.

October 2027

October is often an in-between month. Winter coats and boots are arriving, so they are commonly closer to a new-season price than to clearance. Lower prices for that gear commonly showed up the previous February and March, and they commonly show up again the following February and March. October is the month to buy a coat you need for the winter ahead.

Early holiday promotions sometimes start on electronics. Many of the wider TV discounts still commonly wait for late November, the month after this calendar ends. Mattresses and appliances sometimes show up on the mid-October long weekend, Monday, October 11. Halloween is Sunday, October 31. Costumes and decor for that day often clear in the last days of October and into early November, after the holiday.

Why a sale in the right month can still be fake

You can shop in the month this page suggests and still pay the ordinary price. A sale badge in a busy month is built the same way as a sale badge in a quiet one. The seller raises the list price or the "was" price, then discounts it back to the old everyday price. The percent looks large. The amount due does not change. The calendar does not check that math.

The same gap shows up when the price did fall last month and this month's ad repeats it. The calendar said when to look. A repeated price is not a new low. Seasonal goods can also borrow a percent off a different size, a different model, or a version that almost never sold at the printed reference.

Price history settles it for the exact item. Read the checkout total, not the crossed-out number. Compare it with the low over the last 60 to 90 days, and with the typical price, the level the line sits on most days. Match the count, size, color, condition, and model. A price at or under that recent low is a real candidate. A price that only beats the "was" price is the regular price with a seasonal name.

A short checklist before you buy

Answer these from the calendar and from checkout.

  1. 1Is this a month when this category commonly goes on sale, or am I early or late in the season?
  2. 2What will I pay after codes and shipping, before an unapproved rebate?
  3. 3Is today at or under the 60 to 90 day low for this same item, or only under a "was" price?
  4. 4Is the per-unit price better than a smaller size I would actually use?
  5. 5If this is end of season, is the size, color, and model I want still available?
  6. 6If the recent low is unclear, will I skip the percent off?

When the month fits and checkout is at or under the recent low for the same item, the calendar and the chart agree. If the recent low is unclear, skip the offer.

Questions people ask

When is the best time to buy a TV?

TVs commonly go on sale around Black Friday and Cyber Monday, and often again before a major sporting event people watch at home. In 2026 those named days are Friday, November 27, and Monday, November 30. A sale label in that window can still repeat a recent price, so compare the checkout total with the recent low.

When do laptops usually go on sale?

Laptops commonly go on sale in the back-to-school stretch, often in August and into early September, and again around the late-November holiday weekend. Either month is a reason to look. The lower honest price is the one at or under the recent low for that exact model.

When is a good time to buy winter clothing?

Winter coats, boots, and snow gear are often cheapest at the end of the season, commonly in February and March, when fewer people still need them. Selection is thinner then. Buying in the fall usually means a fuller rack and a price closer to the new-season level.

When do mattresses and appliances usually go on sale?

Mattresses and large appliances often carry sale labels on holiday weekends, including the late-November weekend and the long weekends in mid-February, late May, early July, and early September. The label is common. A real discount still has to come in under the recent price for that same model.

Can a sale in the right month still be fake?

Yes. A seller can raise a was price and discount it back to the ordinary selling price during a month when that category usually goes on sale. The month is a reason to look. Price history is how you tell whether this checkout total is actually low.

What months does this calendar cover?

It runs from November 2026 through October 2027. In 2026, Black Friday is Friday, November 27, and Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30. Similar months tend to be worth a look the following year. Each item still needs its own price check.
