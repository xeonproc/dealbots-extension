Black Friday gaming console and video game deals: what to check before you buy

Updated November 13, 2026

A console or game deal can look like a deep cut and still be an older revision, the smaller storage, or a digital-only edition next to a price people remember from the disc version. The revision in the cart, the storage on the sheet, and this edition's own prices tell you whether the discount is on the console you meant to buy.

In 2026, Black Friday is Friday, November 27, and Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30. Next year the Friday moves. The checks below do not. Which revision and edition is in the cart

A current-generation console and the previous generation can share a family name. A later revision of the current generation can share it too. "New" means the unit has not been owned. Write the full name from the cart, including any word that marks a revision, a storage amount, or a disc drive, and match it to the sheet. If there is no sheet, pause.

Read what this revision changes: size, ports, the disc drive, what is in the box, and the storage. Two storage amounts do not share a price. The headline can be the smaller drive. A large percent off this generation's list price can still sit above the previous generation's selling prices. Each revision and each storage size has its own chart.

Digital-only editions, discs, and resale

Some consoles in a generation are sold as a digital-only edition and as a disc edition. The digital-only edition plays downloads. It has no disc drive, so a disc will not play on it. The disc edition plays a disc and can still download games from the platform's store. They do not share a chart.

A disc you already own works on the edition with a drive. A used disc you might buy later, and a disc you might sell or give away, depend on that drive too. A download stays on the account that bought it. If you keep discs, or you sell games when you finish them, price the disc edition and the discs you will still buy. If every game you will play is a download, price the digital-only edition on its own chart and add those downloads.

Console bundle math

A console bundle adds games, a second controller, a subscription, or store credit to the box. Price this revision and this storage alone. Then add only the pieces you would buy on their own, at a price you can find for each piece sold alone.

Read whether each included game is a disc or a code. A code is a download for an account. A game you already own adds nothing, as a code or as a disc, and so does a controller you already have. How to split the package, including an extra you would not buy, is in Black Friday bundles: real savings or padding?

The platform's online subscription

A console offer can include the platform's online subscription for online play, a catalog of games, cloud saves, or more than one of those. Read which of your games need the plan in this cart, and add that plan only for those games.

Read the price during a discount, the price after it ends, and whether it renews. A trial that renews costs money unless you cancel on the printed terms. A block of months paid in advance is a different offer from a plan that bills again. Add the fee for as long as you would keep it. The saving is the difference, over those periods, against the price you would have paid. A subscription you would not have bought is a new cost, and months you will not use count as zero.

Controllers and other accessories

The photo can show a second controller, a headset, a charging dock, or a camera the cart does not include. If the sheet does not list it, it is not included. Add the price of the ones you will still buy.

A first-party controller is the one from the company that makes the console. A third-party controller, from another company, can cost less and can leave out a feature the first one lists: a particular button, vibration, a headset connection, or wireless play. Read the features on this sheet, and the consoles it names. Read how it charges. A cable, a dock, or batteries you replace are part of the price when the box leaves them out. A controller from another generation works on this console only when both sheets say so. An adapter required to connect it belongs in the total.

Digital game sales and physical prices

The same game can be on sale as a download and as a disc at two checkout prices. A percent off the download is not a percent off the disc. Match the edition in the cart, including an edition whose sheet adds extra content, and read that edition's chart. A code in a bundle is the download. Price it against the download. A disc in the bundle is the physical edition. Price it against the disc.

The number next to "was," "list," "compare at," or a percent off is a reference the seller chose. Checkout and this edition's recent selling prices are the facts. How that reference inflates the percent is in how to tell if a sale is real, and the cluster versus the recent low is in how to read a price history chart. About 30 to 90 days, when the chart has them. If the "was" price never appears as a selling price, the percent is a label, and the game is at its usual price.

Storage expansion

Installed games use the space the system leaves free. Read whether you can add storage later, and what kind this console accepts. A card made for this console, a drive the sheet says you can install, and a drive that only holds games you move by hand are different products. A card or drive the sheet does not accept will not expand this console. Add the price of the expansion you will buy, and of any adapter the sheet requires.

A larger storage edition of the same console is a different product, with its own low. The smaller edition plus a card can cost more than that larger edition. Add both ways, on each edition's own chart, before you treat the smaller one's percent off as the cheaper console.

Handhelds, portable computers, and older stock

A handheld that plays this platform's games, and a portable computer sold for games, are not the console in the family name. Read which store the handheld uses and which of your games it runs. A game tied to the console's account may have to be bought again. A portable computer can need its own store, its own subscription, and its own storage. Price that machine on its own chart, and add the games, controller, or dock that will not carry over.

An older-generation console on a clearance tag is the older machine. Its games, its controllers, and its online subscription can differ from the current generation. If you want the current generation's games, that clearance price is a different product.

Gift cards, refurbished consoles, and returns

A bonus gift card or store credit for buying the console has a face amount. It helps when you would have spent that amount with that seller. A card you will not use is packaging. How to turn the face amount into a usable value, including a spend threshold and a card that expires, is in Black Friday gift card bonuses and 'spend $X, get $Y' promos.

A refurbished or open-box console can be an older revision, a smaller drive, a digital-only edition, a worn battery on a handheld, or a box missing the controller or the cables. Read what was replaced. How those conditions compare is in refurbished vs new. Keep refurbished and new on separate charts. A protection plan in the cart is a second purchase, priced in extended warranties and protection plans.

Opening the box, signing into an account, downloading a game, or breaking a seal on a disc can count as opened. Holiday windows, restocking fees, and final-sale labels are in Black Friday return policies. Use the policy on this cart.

When to wait

Wait if the revision, the storage, the disc drive, or the sheet is missing, or if a subscription you will not keep is in the total. Wait if checkout is only under a reference price. Compare Friday and Monday in Black Friday vs Cyber Monday, and an early label in early Black Friday deals. Buy when the cart matches the sheet and checkout is at or under this edition's recent low. A delivery date is in holiday shipping cutoffs.

A short checklist

Answer these from the cart, the sheet, this edition's prices, and the return terms.

  1. 1Is this the generation and revision I mean, and is it the disc edition or the digital-only edition?
  2. 2Which storage amount is in the cart, and what would the expansion I will actually buy cost?
  3. 3Which included games would I buy anyway, and is each one a code or a disc?
  4. 4Which games need the platform's online subscription, and what do the periods I will keep cost after the discount?
  5. 5Is the controller included, how does it charge, and which consoles does its sheet say it works with?
  6. 6Is the game's checkout price at or under this edition's recent low, download or disc, on its own chart?
  7. 7Does a gift card, store credit, or a refurbished condition change what I am actually buying?
  8. 8What does the return window say once the box is opened, and is checkout at or under this console's recent low?

When those match, the deal is this console and these games.

Questions people ask

How can I tell which console revision is in the cart?

Match the full name in the cart to the sheet: the generation, the revision, the storage amount, and whether it plays discs. An older revision, a smaller drive, and a digital-only edition do not share a chart.

What does a digital-only edition change for games and resale?

A digital-only console plays downloads and has no disc drive. A disc you own, a used disc, and selling a disc apply to the edition with a drive. A download stays on the account that bought it.

How should I count a game included with the console?

Add the console, then only the games and accessories you would buy on their own. Read whether each game is a code or a disc. A game you already own adds nothing.

What does a discounted online subscription actually save?

Read the price during the discount, the price after it ends, and whether it renews. Add only the periods you will keep. The saving is the difference against the price you would have paid.

How do I tell whether a game's sale is its usual price?

Read this edition's recent selling prices, download or disc, on its own chart. A percent off a reference price is a label until shoppers have paid that reference price.

What should I check on a refurbished console and on an opened return?

Read the revision, the storage, what was replaced, and whether the controller and cables are in the box. Opening the box, signing in, or downloading can count as opened, so use the return policy on this cart.
