Black Friday tablet and e-reader deals: what to check before you buy

Updated November 12, 2026

A tablet or e-reader deal can look like a deep cut and still be last year's model, the smallest storage, or an ad-supported version next to a price people remember from the ad-free one. The generation, the storage in the cart, and this model's own prices tell you whether the discount is on the device you meant to buy.

In 2026, Black Friday is Friday, November 27, and Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30. Next year the Friday moves. The checks below do not. Older generations dressed as doorbusters

A current model and last year's model can share a family name. "New" means it has not been owned, not that it is this year's model. Write the full name from the cart, with any number, screen size, year, or suffix, and match it to the sheet. Two sizes do not share a price. Compare storage, Wi-Fi versus cellular, a card slot, and the software window with last year's sheet. If there is no sheet, pause. A large percent off this year's list price can still sit above last year's selling prices. The same gap on a laptop is in Black Friday laptop deals.

Storage tiers, cards, and cloud plans

The headline price can be the smallest storage tier, while larger tiers stay at ordinary prices. Match the amount in the cart to the sheet and the chart. The labeled amount is not free space, because the system keeps some of it. Use a usable-space figure when the sheet lists one. A memory card fits only if the sheet lists a slot. A card left out of the box is another purchase, and it helps only if apps or books can live on it. Audiobooks can fill a small e-reader. Extra cloud space can be a renewing plan. Add that plan only while you would keep it.

Wi-Fi and a cellular data plan

A Wi-Fi model uses a network you already have. A cellular model can add a data plan and a higher checkout price. Read who bills it, whether it shares a phone line you already pay or needs its own, and what canceling costs. A trial that renews costs money unless you cancel on the printed terms. Add the fee for as long as you would keep the plan. If you will not keep one, price the Wi-Fi model on its own chart. An e-reader may be Wi-Fi only, and a cellular version is a separate model. A line fee on a watch is covered in Black Friday smartwatch and fitness tracker deals.

Lock-screen ads and the price to remove them

Some e-readers and tablets are sold with lock-screen offers. The version without them can be another listing, or the cart can charge to turn the offers off. Read whether that charge is permanent and whether you can pay it later. Add it if you will pay it. No printed price means the cost is unknown. The two versions do not share a chart, so a percent off the one with offers is not a discount on the one without them. A kids' edition can have its own rule about offers. Do not assume it is the ad-free model.

E-reader screens: resolution, light, and water

An e-ink screen does not glow like a tablet panel. Resolution, a pixel count or pixels per inch, is how sharp the letters look. A lower figure looks softer on a larger screen. Read whether this panel is color or black and white. A front light lets you read in the dark. A warm light shifts that light to a warmer tone. If the sheet omits one, this model does not list it. A water-resistance rating counts only when this sheet prints it. Another model's rating does not transfer.

Tablet screens: panel and refresh rate

Read the panel type the sheet names, such as LCD or OLED, and the refresh rate. A higher rate can look smoother when you scroll and can use the battery faster. A missing type or rate means you cannot compare them. Resolution is the pixel count. A lower count looks softer on a larger screen. Brightness, when listed, is how well you see the panel in a bright room. A laptop's built-in screen is a different check, in Black Friday laptop deals.

Stylus, keyboard, and bundle math

The photo can show a stylus, a keyboard, or a case the cart does not include. If the sheet does not list it, it is not included. An accessory from another year fits only when this sheet says so. Add the price of the ones you will still buy, and confirm a charger is in the box. Price the tablet alone, then only those accessories. How to split a bundle is in Black Friday bundles: real savings or padding?

How long software updates last

The sheet may say how long this model gets software updates. Silence means the window is unknown. Do not copy a length from another model or from a laptop. Last year's model may be near the end of that window, so read its sheet. An app or bookstore that needs a newer version than this model will get is one you cannot keep. A protection plan does not add updates the sheet does not promise. How to price that plan is in extended warranties and protection plans.

Kids' editions

A kids' edition is its own listing. The extra price can buy a tougher case, a parent account, a content library, or a replacement promise. It is not more storage, a better screen, or cellular service unless this sheet says so. It can be Wi-Fi only when the ordinary model is not. Price the ordinary model plus a case you would buy, and compare. Read what a replacement promise covers, how many times, and how long. A library on a paid plan is a separate cost. Add it only while you would keep it.

Stores, formats, and lock-in

A book bought in one store's app may open only there. A file you can download and move is a different purchase. Read the formats this e-reader opens. One it does not list is a book you may have to buy again. A tablet's apps come from its own store, and it does not run another store's apps. Match the store to what you already pay for. For a gift, match the recipient's store. A lower price in the wrong store is a different product. The discount does not move your library.

This model's price history

The number next to "was," "list," "compare at," or a percent off is a reference the seller chose. Checkout, and this model's recent selling prices, are the facts. How that reference inflates the percent is in how to tell if a sale is real. Read the chart for this year, size, storage, connection, and ad version. Each of those, and a kids' edition, has its own low. New, open-box, and refurbished do not share a chart. About 30 to 90 days, when the chart has them. The cluster is the typical price and the bottom is the recent low, as in how to read a price history chart. If the "was" price never appears as a selling price, the percent is a label.

Refurbished units and the return window

A refurbished or open-box unit can be an older year, less storage, a worn battery, or a box missing the charger, stylus, or keyboard. Read what was replaced. How those conditions compare, including a battery that may not have been replaced, is in refurbished vs new. Keep them on separate charts. Opening the box or signing in can count as opened. Holiday windows, fees, and final-sale labels are in Black Friday return policies. Use the policy on this cart.

When to wait

Wait if the generation, the storage, the ad version, or the sheet is missing. Wait if a data plan you will not keep is in the total, if your books or apps will not open here, or if the stylus, keyboard, and charger are unpriced extras. Wait if checkout is only under a reference price. Compare Friday and Monday in Black Friday vs Cyber Monday, and an early label in early Black Friday deals. Buy when the cart matches the sheet and the chart and checkout is at or under this model's recent low. A delivery date is in holiday shipping cutoffs.

A short checklist

Answer these from the cart, the sheet, this model's prices, and the return terms.

  1. 1Is this the generation I mean, last year's model, or a sale-only variant?
  2. 2Which storage amount is in the cart, can I add a card, and would a cloud plan be required?
  3. 3Is this Wi-Fi or cellular, and what does the data plan cost if I keep it?
  4. 4Is this the ad-supported version, and what does this cart charge to remove the offers?
  5. 5What screen does the sheet list: e-ink resolution, front light, warm light, and water resistance, or panel type and refresh rate?
  6. 6Are the stylus, keyboard, case, and charger included, or do I still have to buy them?
  7. 7How long does this model receive software updates, and will my books and apps open in this store?
  8. 8Is checkout at or under this model's recent low, and what do the return window and the warranty say?

When those match, the deal is this device. A chart for another year, storage tier, or ad version does not apply.

Questions people ask

How can I tell this generation from last year's model?

Match the full model name in the cart to the sheet and compare storage, screen size, Wi-Fi versus cellular, and the software window. A matching photo can still be last year's tablet or e-reader.

Is the base storage the one on sale, and can I add more later?

The sale can be only the smallest tier. Read whether a memory card fits and whether apps can live on it. A cloud plan is a separate cost. Another tier's low belongs to that tier.

What do I keep paying for on a cellular tablet?

A data plan can add a fee for as long as you keep it, on top of checkout. If you will not keep the plan, price the Wi-Fi model on its own chart.

What does an ad-supported e-reader change?

Offers can sit on the lock screen. The ad-free version can cost more, or the cart can charge to turn the offers off. Those are different products and they do not share a chart.

Are the stylus and keyboard included in the sale price?

Only when the cart and the sheet say so. A photo can show accessories the box does not include. Add the ones you will still buy, and price a bundle by the pieces you would buy alone.

Will the books and apps I already own work on this device?

Books can be locked to one store's format, and apps come from this tablet's store. Read the formats and the store on this sheet. A discount does not move a library to a store that cannot open it.
