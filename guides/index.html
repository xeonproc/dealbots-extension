Shopping guides

Updated November 24, 2026

A sale label is a claim. These guides are the checks behind it: the checkout price, the recent low, and the fine print.

The first guides are for early November, when stores are already using the Black Friday name and the named Friday is still weeks away. One decides whether to buy that early price now or wait. The next is how an early label works, and which kinds of products often move before the weekend. In 2026, Black Friday is Friday, November 27, and Cyber Monday is Monday, November 30. DealBots is a free browser extension that checks price history on one pick a day. The pick comes from what deal-hunter communities are upvoting. A price that only looks low next to a fake "was" price is filtered out, and the pick explains why it made the cut. There is no account and no tracking.

  1. Early Black Friday deals: buy now or wait?

    Stores put a Black Friday label on prices weeks before November 27, 2026, and price history is how you decide what to buy now and what to leave until the weekend.

  2. Countdown timers and 'only 3 left' badges: is Black Friday urgency real?

    A countdown, a low-stock badge, or a sale-ends-tonight banner rushes the decision. Price history is how you tell a real low from the ordinary price with a clock on it.

  3. Are Black Friday deals actually real?

    Some doorbusters are a real low, and others repeat a recent selling price under a holiday label, so compare checkout with the recent low, the model number, and the return rules.

  4. Black Friday bundles: real savings or padding?

    A bundle can be a lower checkout total or padding on extras you would not buy. Price each piece, the main item's history, and the per-unit cost.

  5. Black Friday TV deals: why the model number doesn't match (and how to compare)

    A Black Friday television can share a series name with a reviewed set and carry a different model number. Compare the spec sheet, and read that model's own price history.

  6. Refurbished vs new on Black Friday: when refurbished is the better deal

    A refurbished, open-box, or renewed Black Friday price can beat new, or lose once new is actually on sale. Compare the real gap, the warranty, and the new item's typical price.

  7. Black Friday laptop deals: the specs to check before you buy

    A doorbuster laptop can be cheap because the specs are thin. Check the memory, storage, processor, screen, full model number, and that model's own price history.

  8. Black Friday headphone and earbud deals: what to check before you buy

    A headphone or earbud deal can be last year's model, a thinner feature list, or a list price the pair rarely sold at. Check the specs, that model's price history, and the return window for fit.

  9. Black Friday kitchen appliance deals (air fryers, blenders, stand mixers, coffee makers): what to check before you buy

    A doorbuster air fryer, blender, stand mixer, or coffee maker can be a seller-exclusive model or a bundle of extras. Check capacity, wattage, parts, footprint, warranty, and that model's own price history.

  10. Black Friday monitor deals: what to check before you buy

    A doorbuster monitor can be a softer picture, a refresh-rate claim, or a sale-only model. Check pixel density, the panel, ports, the stand, the full model number, and that model's own price history.

  11. Black Friday mattress deals: what to check before you buy

  12. Black Friday phone deals: unlocked vs carrier, activation strings, and trade-ins

  13. Black Friday robot vacuum deals: what to check before you buy

    An e-bike or electric scooter percent-off can be a smaller battery, a range measured under ideal conditions, or a list price this model has not been selling at. Check the watt-hours, the class, and that model's own prices.

  28. Black Friday gift card bonuses and 'spend $X, get $Y' promos: are they worth it?

    A bonus gift card or a spend-and-get offer is worth the part you will actually spend. Compare that usable value with a straight discount, and check the base price first.

  29. Extended warranties and protection plans on Black Friday: are they worth it?

    A protection plan at checkout is a second purchase. Compare it with the warranty you already have, any card coverage that overlaps, and whether the add-on spends the discount.

  30. Black Friday vs Cyber Monday: which one has the real deals?

    Neither named day is automatically cheaper, so compare the item you want on both days with the price it has actually been selling for.

    A want list, a cap on what you will pay, and a note of the normal price turn the weekend into a comparison you can make at checkout.

  32. The price dropped right after I bought it. Can I get the difference back?

    The price dropped after you bought it. A price adjustment, a return and rebuy, or card price protection may cover the difference, and each one depends on the current terms.

  33. Black Friday return policies: windows, restocking fees, and holiday exceptions

    A holiday return window can differ from the usual one. Check restocking fees, opened items, final sale, gift receipts, and in-store versus online returns before checkout.

  34. Holiday shipping cutoffs: buy now or risk a late gift?

    A gift has a date it has to be in hand. Read the order-by, ship-by, and deliver-by dates, the shipping method, and what a missed guarantee pays before you buy on price alone.
